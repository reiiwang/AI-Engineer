# Nimbus Labs Company Overview - Summary

## Company profile

[Nimbus Labs](../entity/nimbus-labs.md) is a fictional infrastructure software company founded in Taipei in 2019 by [Lin Mei-Hua](../entity/lin-mei-hua.md) and [Daniel Ortega](../entity/daniel-ortega.md). It builds data infrastructure for AI applications, focusing on search over unstructured data. The company employs about 140 people across offices in Taipei and Berlin.

## History and development

Before founding the company, [Lin Mei-Hua](../entity/lin-mei-hua.md) led the search infrastructure team at a large e-commerce company. Her experience with the limitations of keyword search for product discovery helped motivate the venture. [Daniel Ortega](../entity/daniel-ortega.md), a database researcher working on approximate nearest neighbor indexes, brought related research expertise. They founded [Nimbus Labs](../entity/nimbus-labs.md) to develop that research into a product.

Key milestones:

- **2019:** Founded; raised a USD 4 million seed round.
- **2021:** Released [Cirrus](../entity/cirrus.md), its [Vector Database](../concept/vector-database.md).
- **2023:** Raised a USD 38 million Series B led by Northwind Ventures and opened a Berlin office.
- **2024:** Launched [Cirrus Cloud](../entity/cirrus-cloud.md), a managed version of [Cirrus](../entity/cirrus.md).

## Products and business model

[Nimbus Labs](../entity/nimbus-labs.md) offers two products:

| Product | Description | License |
|---|---|---|
| [Cirrus](../entity/cirrus.md) | Open-source [Vector Database](../concept/vector-database.md) for similarity search | Apache 2.0 |
| [Cirrus Cloud](../entity/cirrus-cloud.md) | Fully managed service based on [Cirrus](../entity/cirrus.md), with usage-based pricing | Commercial |

The products serve complementary roles in the company’s business model. The open-source [Cirrus](../entity/cirrus.md) project is the main adoption channel: teams can begin by self-hosting the database and later move to the managed service. Most of the company’s revenue comes from [Cirrus Cloud](../entity/cirrus-cloud.md).

## Customers and use cases

[Nimbus Labs](../entity/nimbus-labs.md) reports more than 600 paying customers. Its most common use case is [Retrieval-Augmented Generation](../concept/retrieval-augmented-generation.md) (RAG): an application retrieves relevant documents from [Cirrus](../entity/cirrus.md) and supplies them to a large language model as context. Other reported uses include product recommendation and duplicate image detection.

## Leadership

- [Lin Mei-Hua](../entity/lin-mei-hua.md), co-founder and CEO, is responsible for product direction and go-to-market.
- [Daniel Ortega](../entity/daniel-ortega.md), co-founder and CTO, leads the core database engineering team.
- [Priya Raman](../entity/priya-raman.md) has served as VP of Engineering since 2022. Previously at a cloud storage company, she owns reliability for [Cirrus Cloud](../entity/cirrus-cloud.md).

## Key Takeaways

- [Nimbus Labs](../entity/nimbus-labs.md) combines open-source database adoption with a commercial managed service.
- [Cirrus](../entity/cirrus.md) supports similarity search over data, while [Cirrus Cloud](../entity/cirrus-cloud.md) provides the managed offering and generates most company revenue.
- [Retrieval-Augmented Generation](../concept/retrieval-augmented-generation.md) is the company’s most common customer use case.
- The company grew from its 2019 founding to a 2023 Series B, a Berlin office, and more than 600 reported paying customers.
