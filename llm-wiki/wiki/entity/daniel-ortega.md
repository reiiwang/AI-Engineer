# Daniel Ortega

Daniel Ortega is a co-founder and CTO of [Nimbus Labs](nimbus-labs.md). He leads the core database engineering team.

He was a database researcher working on approximate nearest neighbor indexes. He co-founded [Nimbus](nimbus.md) Labs in 2019 with [Lin Mei-Hua](lin-mei-hua.md) to turn that research into a product.
