# Lin Mei-Hua

Lin Mei-Hua is a co-founder and CEO of [Nimbus Labs](nimbus-labs.md). She leads the company's product direction and go-to-market activities.

Before founding [Nimbus](nimbus.md) Labs, she led the search infrastructure team at a large e-commerce company, where she encountered the limits of keyword search for product discovery. She co-founded Nimbus Labs with [Daniel Ortega](daniel-ortega.md) in 2019.
