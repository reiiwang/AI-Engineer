# Nimbus Labs Company Overview

Nimbus Labs is a fictional infrastructure software company founded in 2019 in Taipei by Lin Mei-Hua and Daniel Ortega. The company builds data infrastructure for AI applications, with a focus on search over unstructured data. Nimbus Labs employs about 140 people across offices in Taipei and Berlin.

## History

Lin Mei-Hua previously led the search infrastructure team at a large e-commerce company, where she ran into the limits of keyword search for product discovery. Daniel Ortega was a database researcher working on approximate nearest neighbor indexes. They started Nimbus Labs in 2019 to turn that research into a product.

- **2019**: Company founded; seed round of USD 4 million.
- **2021**: First release of Cirrus, the company's vector database.
- **2023**: Series B of USD 38 million led by Northwind Ventures; Berlin office opened.
- **2024**: Launched Cirrus Cloud, a managed version of Cirrus.

## Products

Nimbus Labs has two products:

| Product | Description | License |
|---|---|---|
| Cirrus | Open-source vector database for similarity search | Apache 2.0 |
| Cirrus Cloud | Fully managed Cirrus service with usage-based pricing | Commercial |

Most revenue comes from Cirrus Cloud. The open-source Cirrus project is used as the main adoption channel: teams start self-hosting and later move to the managed service.

## Customers and use cases

Nimbus Labs reports more than 600 paying customers. The most common use case is Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), where an application retrieves relevant documents from Cirrus and passes them to a large language model as context. Other use cases include product recommendation and duplicate image detection.

## Leadership

- **Lin Mei-Hua**: Co-founder and CEO. Responsible for product direction and go-to-market.
- **Daniel Ortega**: Co-founder and CTO. Leads the core database engineering team.
- **Priya Raman**: VP of Engineering since 2022, previously at a cloud storage company. She owns Cirrus Cloud reliability.
